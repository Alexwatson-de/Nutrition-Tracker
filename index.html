    How calculations work

    Nutrition consumed = nutrition per 100 g × quantity in grams ÷ 100. For example, 150 g of cooked rice at 130 kcal / 100 g = 195 kcal.

    This version uses reliable manual calculations from the values you enter — there is no AI food recognition or automatic nutrition analysis. Built-in database values are approximate examples; check packaging or a nutrition database for accuracy.

    NutriLens AI stores data only in your browser (localStorage). It provides no medical or dietary advice — example targets are not suitable for everyone. Consult a qualified professional for personal nutrition guidance.
